This plugin submits your IP address, questions and chat history to OpenAI using your authorized ChatGPT plan. Optional Wiki searches send search terms and page titles to the OSRS Wiki; optional Wise Old Man lookups send your character name to Wise Old Man. Account details are shared with OpenAI only under the separate account-sharing settings. These third-party servers are not controlled or verified by RuneLite developers. ChatGPT sign-in is retained locally using Windows account encryption.
