GETTING STARTED
==============
Guide revision: 2026-10-15
Project: https://github.com/D-AZ0617/osrs-ai-buddy

Welcome to OSRS AI Buddy, an open-source companion inside RuneLite.
Use it to plan training, choose account goals, prepare for quests or bosses,
and understand what to do next using the account details you choose to share.

Buddy gives advice. It does not click, move your character, fight, trade,
or otherwise play the game for you. Treat its answers as suggestions and
check important requirements against the OSRS Wiki.

1. YOUR FIRST CONVERSATION
--------------------------
- Open OSRS AI Buddy from RuneLite's sidebar.
- The starting screen is Conversations. Your existing chats appear as cards.
- Open the three-dot menu and choose Sign In to connect your ChatGPT plan.
- Complete authorization on OpenAI's website. Review its permissions and
  usage limits there. Buddy does not ask for an API key.
- Press + when YOU want to start a new conversation.
- Choose an available model beside Send, then type a question and send it.
- Suggested starting questions fill the input only. They never send a message.

A new conversation remains an unsaved draft until you send its first message.
Repeated + clicks reuse an empty draft instead of creating numbered chats.
Returning to Conversations does not add an unsent draft to your history.
The first sent question becomes the conversation's initial title.

Try questions such as:
  "Use my shared account progress to suggest my next three goals."
  "What should I finish before attempting my first raid?"
  "Check the Wiki for a training method that fits my levels."
  "Which items are missing from the collection-log page I just opened?"

2. FINDING AND MANAGING CONVERSATIONS
-------------------------------------
Each conversation card shows its title, latest message date/time and a short
preview. Click a card or Open Conversation to return to it. Click the title
at the top of an open chat to return to Conversations.

With no conversation history, a recessed gray message says:
  Future conversations will appear here.

The three-dot menu includes:
  Rename          Give the open conversation a more useful title.
  Clear           Remove the conversation's messages and AI summary.
  Delete          Remove the conversation and return to Conversations.
  Configurations  Open Buddy's settings and Quick Access links.
  ChatGPT status  Show whether your account is connected.
  Sign In/Log Out  Connect or disconnect your ChatGPT account.

Deleting a chat never creates another one and never opens an older chat.
Old, empty automatic "Chat 1", "Chat 2", etc. placeholders are cleaned up.
Conversations containing messages are preserved.

3. CONFIGURATIONS: CHOOSE WHAT BUDDY CAN SEE
--------------------------------------------
Account sharing is off by default. Enable only the details you want to use.
These settings affect new lookups; they cannot retract information already
sent to a service or remove information from existing chat history.

ACCOUNT CONTEXT
  Share Character Overview
    Shares your character name, account type, levels, quest progress, and
    requested Slayer-task and achievement-diary information with OpenAI.

  Share Achievements & Collections
    Allows account overviews, skill XP and boosted levels, quest states,
    known combat-achievement flags, collection-log observations, selected
    prayer/Slayer unlocks and selected currencies to be used as context.

  Share Bank, Inventory & Gear
    Allows equipment, current inventory and the last observed bank snapshot.
    Open your bank with sharing enabled to make its contents available.
    An unopened or unseen bank is unknown, not empty.

KNOWLEDGE SOURCES
  Allow OSRS Wiki Searches
    Allows relevant Wiki searches and page reads. Search terms can reflect
    your question and anything shared with Buddy. Results can include links.
    GE price lookups use RuneLite's price data.

  Use Wise Old Man History
    Reads your existing public WOM profile, tracked skills/XP, boss kills,
    activities, gains, records, achievements and related tracking history.
    It does not create tracking, update a profile or upload your live game
    progress to WOM. Missing or unranked data does not mean zero progress.
    WOM can be older than your current in-game account state.

Buddy replies stay in the sidebar. Reply alerts and game-chat echoes are disabled.

4. COLLECTION LOGS: KNOWN, MISSING AND UNKNOWN
---------------------------------------------
A completed or incomplete collection section is not the same thing as knowing
every individual item in it.

With Share Achievements & Collections enabled, open your OWN collection log
and browse the pages relevant to your question. Buddy observes each page's
obtained/missing item status, quantities, page name and observation time.
It checks that the page header totals agree with the item grid before
accepting a snapshot, to avoid using partially loaded pages.

After observing a page, you can close the log and ask about those items during
the same session. Unvisited pages/items remain UNKNOWN, not automatically
missing. Missing status describes the observed snapshot, which may become old.
House adventure-log views are excluded to avoid reading someone else's log.

These observations stay in memory. Account transitions, disabling sharing,
or shutting down Buddy clear them. Existing chat messages may still contain
previously shared details. WOM does not supply a private item-level log.

5. YOUR CHATGPT CONNECTION
---------------------------
On Windows, Buddy remembers your authorized session using encryption tied
to your Windows user account. Sign in once in the updated build; ordinary
restarts should restore the session and refresh access tokens when needed.

There is no separate "allow answers" or "remember login" checkbox.
Signing out stops requests, attempts to revoke the renewable session and
clears the locally saved tokens. Revoked access or expiry of the renewable
session can still require another sign-in. On other operating systems,
persistent protection is not implemented yet and sign-in is session-only.

Buddy uses your authorized ChatGPT plan. It does not silently fall back to a
separately billed OpenAI API key. Eligibility, plan limits and any credit
permissions are controlled by OpenAI. Review app access and usage at:
  https://chatgpt.com/settings/usage

6. LOCAL FILES AND QUICK ACCESS
--------------------------------
Conversations are saved automatically on this PC. There is no save-history
checkbox. Clear or Delete chats in Buddy to remove history.

Saved Conversations
  Opens conversations/, with one readable Markdown (.md) transcript per chat
  and README.md as an index. Titles, timestamps and message formatting are kept.
  These files can contain personal messages and shared account information.
  Generated transcripts are refreshed automatically; edits are not imported.
  Clear or Delete in Buddy updates/removes the generated copy. Do not post it publicly.
  Long chats may be summarized; the saved format keeps the latest 200
  messages plus older messages still needed for the conversation.

Installed Plugin Folder
  Opens the folder containing Buddy's installed JAR. A developer build may
  instead show compiled classes. The button never executes the JAR.

Getting Started
  Opens this guide as buddy-information.txt in Buddy's data folder.
  Opening the guide refreshes this generated file to the current build's
  help text. It is not intended as a place for personal notes.

GitHub Repository
  Opens source code, documentation and updates in your web browser.

Other local files:
  chats.json              Formatted JSON used internally to restore chats.
  conversations/          Readable copies generated from saved chat history.
  chats.lock              Prevents competing writers to conversation history.
  chatgpt/                Contains registration data and the protected session.
  buddy-information.txt   This generated help guide.

Do not share the chatgpt subfolder. Use Log Out to clear the saved session.
Programs running as your Windows user may also access that user's session.
Only one RuneLite window can own Buddy's saved chats at a time. If another
window owns them, Buddy reports that it cannot save this window's history.

7. PRIVACY AND DATA SOURCES
----------------------------
Questions, relevant chat history/summaries and permitted tool results go to
OpenAI. Wiki searches go to the OSRS Wiki. Enabled WOM lookups send your
current character name to Wise Old Man; results may then be used by OpenAI.
Each contacted service can see your IP address.

These services are not controlled or verified by RuneLite developers.
There is no project-operated relay server. Buddy never reads or sends your
Jagex password or launcher login credentials.

Full privacy details:
  https://github.com/D-AZ0617/osrs-ai-buddy/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

8. TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------
"Not connected" / cannot send
  Use Sign In from the chat menu and choose an available model. If a token
  was revoked or its renewable session expired, reconnect through Sign In.

No models in the list
  Confirm ChatGPT is connected, then use the model picker's refresh action.
  Availability is determined by the authorized OpenAI account/workspace.

Buddy cannot see my account details
  Log in to the game and enable the relevant sharing setting. Ask Buddy to
  use its tools instead of guessing. Opening the bank/log can supply data.

WOM is missing, stale or rate limited
  The account needs existing WOM tracking. This plugin does not create it.
  Requests are briefly cached and spaced to respect the free service.
  Rate-limit responses impose a cooldown; try again later.

No saved conversation file yet
  Press + and send your first message. Allow a moment for the save to finish.
  Empty unsent drafts do not produce saved conversation entries.

Changes do not appear in a developer client
  Restart the developer build. The regular Jagex-launched RuneLite cannot
  load this unpublished custom plugin until it is accepted into Plugin Hub.
  Development login may require a temporary local Jagex credential handoff;
  that is separate from Buddy's saved ChatGPT authorization.

The assistant gives a wrong answer
  Ask it to check the Wiki and state its sources. Account observations and
  public trackers can be incomplete or old; AI answers are not guarantees.

LIGHTWEIGHT MODELS
Buddy offers account-listed Luna, GPT-5 mini, Sol and Astra models.
Luna is the default and labeled (Recommended); saved selections are respected.
Custom model names are blocked. Flagship models require explicit selection. If no supported model is listed, refresh the
list; sending pauses instead of falling back to a flagship. This reduces
accidental heavyweight use, but does not reserve quota or guarantee unlimited
messages. ChatGPT controls shared plan limits and model availability.


TOPIC GUIDANCE
Buddy answers RuneScape, genuine math, and Buddy/RuneLite help questions.
For unrelated requests it redirects: I'm here to help with RuneScape. What can I help you work out?
It should not look up or answer unrelated topics, even when asked to ignore
its rules. Mixed requests are answered only for the supported parts. This is
model guidance, not a guaranteed filter; refusals still use plan allowance.



SUBSCRIPTION AND USAGE TROUBLESHOOTING
If your last message cannot be submitted, check that your paid ChatGPT
subscription is active and permitted for the selected account/workspace.
Review https://chatgpt.com/settings/usage for plan, credit and Buddy app limits.
If a limit has been reached, wait for the allowance to reset, adjust the app
limit, or purchase additional ChatGPT credits if offered in those settings.
Separate OpenAI API credits do not apply to this plugin. Temporary service
failures or rate limits do not automatically mean your credits are exhausted.
For an expired session, use Sign In in Buddy's menu.
